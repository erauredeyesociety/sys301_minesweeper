| [firmware-integrity-proof.md](./firmware-integrity-proof.md) | 2026-08-27 | **PROOF that the hub's firmware was never modified**, not the assertion. The crux, stated early: the *firmware* is the MicroPython binary in the STM32F413's internal program flash; `/flash` is the FAT filesystem that firmware **exposes**, and writing a `.py` there is saving a document — it goes *through* the thing it would have to modify. A baseline was captured before anything was written, re-captured after, and diffed: the **complete** difference is four lines — a new `lib` directory, **8 blocks (32 KB, ~0.1% of free space)** consumed, and battery 7942→8001 mV / temperature 247→251 because the hub was charging over USB. **Every stock file byte-identical; identity, module list and API surface unchanged.** Every line of the diff is walked through, including the 3 of 8 blocks that arithmetic does **not** account for. Lists by name what was never done (no DFU, no bootloader, no `machine.reset`, no `vfs.mkfs`, no format, no factory reset, no update prompt, no LEGO app), gives the re-runnable capture+diff commands, and tells a future reader exactly what to do if the diff is ever dirty — a hunk in `01`/`02`/`03` is the firmware moving: stop and get the operator |
| [imu-characterisation-2026-08-27.md](./imu-characterisation-2026-08-27.md) | 2026-08-27 | **MEASURED on our hub — the IMU's units, wrap, read cost and drift.** Units *derived from gravity, not from a datasheet*: a still hub reads `|a| = 989.3`, so `acceleration()` is in **milli-g** (~989 per g); true tilt 0.705° against a reported 6.7 gives ratio **9.53**, so `tilt_angles()` is in **decidegrees** — `hub_imu`'s `/10.0` and `telemetry`'s `*_ddeg` columns are confirmed right, and `accx/y/z` should become `*_mg`. **Yaw wraps at ±180°** (observed −1795 / +1771 ddeg), so every heading delta must go through `odometry.normalize_angle()`. A full IMU tick costs **1.350 ms** = 14 % of a 10 ms budget — but ⚠ **an unresolved anomaly**: the three calls sum to 0.328 ms individually, **4× less** than measured together, so a repeated read may be returning a *cached* value. Plan with 1.350 ms; never quote the per-call figures as read rates. **Drift: 0.0033 °/s over one clean 30 s stationary window** — and a 30 s run showing 3.29 °/s was **DISCARDED** because the drift reversed sign while the operator handled the robot. Drift while *driving*, and over a run-length window, is still **UNMEASURED**. Closes no `config.py` value |

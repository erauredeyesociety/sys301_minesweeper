## The two numbers to remember

- **−2 Schrute Bucks per role violation.** The narrowest trap: the Programmer may touch the robot **only**
  to plug it into or unplug it from their laptop — not to adjust a sensor.
- **1 Schrute Buck per person per minute** for face-to-face beyond the daily 5-minute standup. A
  four-person ten-minute meeting is 40 SB out of a 56 SB balance. Written communication is free and
  unlimited.

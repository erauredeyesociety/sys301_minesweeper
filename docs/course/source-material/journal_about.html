Instructions per Introductory Project:

Journal (80 points): You will spend the last 5-7 minutes each day independently writing a reflection on your performance that day. Each day there will be a posted question of the day (QOD) on the board (in class). When you finish answering the question of the day (QOD) summarize the daily events including but not limited to the following: What went well? What didn't go well? What unforeseen problems arose? How could you have avoided them? What frustrated you? How did your teammates do? This will be submitted at the end of the intro project. Grading will be as follows:

Journal Entry Each Day (-5 points per missing journal entry) 30 points
Entries clearly answer question asked (not so general as to be meaningless) (-5 points per violation) 20 points
Entries more than trivial effort (>= 4 sentences for Question of the Day (QOD), >= 4 sentences for summary of daily events, 8 sentences total) (-5 points per violation) 20 points
Entry Grammar/Clarity 10 points

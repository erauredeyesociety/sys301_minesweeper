**The journal alone outweighs the peer evaluations, and it is scored on showing up and writing eight
real sentences — not on whether the robot works.** It is the cheapest guaranteed score in the project.

2. **An uncollected communications record.** Not reconstructable after the fact; export from week 1.
   → [team/communications.md](./team/communications.md)
